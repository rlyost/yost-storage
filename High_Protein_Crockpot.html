Step 1 — Daily calories

Current body weight (lbs) × 11–12 = daily calories for fat loss. 200 lbs × 12 = 2,400/day; 180 lbs × 11 = 1,980/day. Higher end if you are very active, lower end if sedentary. Track your weight 7–14 days; if it is not moving, drop 150–200 calories and reassess.

Step 2 — Protein goal

Current body weight (lbs) × 0.8–1.0. At 180 lbs that is 144–180g/day; at 200 lbs, 160–200g/day. When in doubt, aim higher.

Step 3 — Get a digital food scale

Eyeballing food is the fastest way to stall progress. Weigh proteins, measure carbs and fats, and learn true portion sizes.

Step 4 — Track daily

Log meals, snacks, sauces and oils in MyFitnessPal, Cronometer or Lose It. Consistency beats perfection.

Crockpot BBQ Beef Roast

520cal
35gprotein
38gfat
10gcarbs
8servings
estimated

Serves 6 to 8

Ingredients

  • 3 to 4 pound chuck roast
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 1 cup Ray's No Sugar Added BBQ sauce
  • ½ cup beef broth
  • 2 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper

Instructions

  1. Place the sliced onion in the crockpot.
  2. Season the roast with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika. Place it over the onion.
  3. Mix the Ray's No Sugar Added BBQ sauce, beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, and vinegar. Pour it over the roast.
  4. Cook on LOW for 8 to 10 hours or HIGH for 5 to 6 hours. The beef is ready when it pulls apart easily with a fork.
  5. Remove the roast and shred it. Discard large pieces of fat.
  6. Return the beef to the crockpot. Stir it into the sauce and cook for another 15 minutes.

Serve on toasted hamburger buns, baked potatoes, rice, or mashed potatoes. Add coleslaw and pickles for sandwiches.

Here is a direct, reliable crockpot beef roast with potatoes and carrots.

Ingredients

  • 3 to 4 lb beef chuck roast
  • 1 lb baby potatoes or 4 large potatoes cut into chunks
  • 4 to 5 carrots sliced thick
  • 1 onion sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried thyme or rosemary

Optional

  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste

Steps

  1. Heat a skillet over medium high heat. Add oil.
  2. Season roast on all sides with salt pepper paprika.
  3. Sear roast 2 to 3 minutes per side until browned.
  4. Place potatoes, carrots and onion in a crockpot.
  5. Set roast on top of vegetables.
  6. Add garlic broth and optional Worcestershire or tomato paste.
  7. Cover and cook.

Cook Time

  • Low 8 hours for tender pull apart beef
  • High 4 to 5 hours if needed faster

Finish

  • Taste and adjust salt.
  • Slice or shred beef.
  • Serve with vegetables and broth as gravy.

If you want thicker gravy or added mushrooms or different seasoning profile, say so and I will adjust the recipe.

Slow Cooker Sesame Ginger Beef

Servings and nutrition not provided in the source.

About 10 minutes to put together, with beef that cooks until fall-apart tender.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds beef stew meat
  • ½ cup low-sodium soy sauce or tamari
  • ⅓ cup packed brown sugar
  • ¼ cup beef broth
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1½ tablespoons freshly grated ginger
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes, or more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch

Steps

  1. Add the beef to the slow cooker, followed by the soy sauce, brown sugar, beef broth, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, rice vinegar, and red pepper flakes.
  2. Cover and cook on low for 8–9 hours or high for 4–5 hours, until the beef is fall-apart tender.
  3. Remove about ¼ to ⅓ cup of the cooking liquid and whisk in the cornstarch until completely smooth. Pour it back into the slow cooker, cover, and cook on high for another 20–30 minutes to thicken the sauce.

Cook Time

Low 8–9 hours or high 4–5 hours, plus 20–30 minutes on high to thicken.

Serving Suggestions

Serve over jasmine rice with green onions, sesame seeds, and something green. Add chili crisp if you like a little heat.

Crockpot Creamy Potato Hamburger Soup

360cal
17gprotein
22gfat
21gcarbs
6servings
from the recipe

A comforting and creamy crockpot soup filled with tender potatoes, seasoned ground beef, and flavorful vegetables. Perfect for a warm, hearty meal with minimal effort.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 5 cups potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 medium carrots, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped (optional, for garnish)

Directions

In a skillet over medium heat, cook the ground beef until browned. Drain excess fat.

Add the diced onion and garlic to the skillet. Cook for 2-3 minutes until fragrant. Transfer the mixture to the crockpot.

Add potatoes, chicken broth, carrots, celery, salt, black pepper, and smoked paprika to the crockpot. Stir to combine.

Cover and cook on low for 6-7 hours or high for 3-4 hours, until potatoes are tender.

About 30 minutes before serving, stir in milk, heavy cream, and shredded cheddar cheese. Let cook until the cheese melts and the soup is creamy.

Ladle soup into bowls, garnish with chopped parsley if desired, and serve hot.

Prep Time: 20 minutes | Cooking Time: 6 hours (low) or 3 hours (high) | Total Time: 6 hours 20 minutes (low) or 3 hours 20 minutes (high)

Ingredients

  • ▢1 tsp each paprika, garlic powder, onion powder + Italian herb blend
  • ▢1 medium onion, diced
  • ▢2 medium carrots, diced
  • ▢3 large portabello caps
  • ▢3 cloves garlic, minced
  • ▢2 tbs tomato paste
  • ▢1 tbs low sodium soy sauce
  • ▢1 tbs Worcestershire sauce

  • ▢Top with: sour cream, olive oil, fresh parsley + freshly grated parmesan, (optional)

Instructions

Heat 1 tbsp of olive oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Once hot, add in the meat and let it stand and sear for at least 1 minute. Season with salt, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, Italian herb blend. Use a wooden spoon to break up the meat until it is no longer pink.

Next, add in the onion, carrots, mushrooms and remaining 2 tbs oil and sauté for 4-5 minutes. Stir in the garlic for 30 seconds. Add in the tomato paste and stir to coat all for 1 minute. Mix in soy sauce, Worcestershire and brown sugar. Lastly, add in the cabbage, tomatoes, beef broth, stir and bring to a boil. Cover the pot with the lid and simmer on low heat - cook for 25 minutes.   4. I used 1 small head of cabbage for the diced 5 cups of cabbage. Leave out the stem.
  5. If you're on a Gluten Free diet, make sure you use Tamari instead of Soy Sauce.

Juicy ground beef, sautéed bell peppers and onions, melty provolone, and a creamy egg mixture bake together into the perfect low-carb, weeknight-friendly dish. Fast prep, hearty flavor, and always a crowd-pleaser.

Prep Time: 15 min

Cook Time: 45 min

Total Time: 1 hour

Servings: 4–6

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground beef
  • 2 bell peppers, chopped
  • 1½ onions, chopped
  • 8 oz provolone cheese, shredded or sliced
  • 6 large eggs
  • ½ cup heavy cream
  • 2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp olive oil or butter for sautéing
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • Optional: 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • Optional: 1–2 jalapeños, diced

Instructions

1️⃣ Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish.
