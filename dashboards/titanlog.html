EVERY WEIGH-IN

Body weight trend

Reading7 day trend
View all readings

Weight comes from each completed workout’s body weight snapshot plus your current weight in TitanLog, read from titanlog-data.js and merged with the newest backup built into this page. When a workout reuses the previous weight it is shown once. The trend is the average of every reading in the trailing 7 days. Uses the Weight unit selector above. Independent of the period filter.

Body weight is stored in kg and converted to your selected weight unit. Measurement units for waist, arms, and thighs are not declared in this backup, so their values are shown as exported.
